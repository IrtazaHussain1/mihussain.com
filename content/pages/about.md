My recent work includes PermisPop, Omnidots, Power Insight, Saleshoney, and platforms at Strategic Systems International. I focus on clear architecture, maintainable code, and the operational side of shipping, monitoring, migrations, and handoffs teams can actually use, so what I build keeps running long after launch.
